> [!recipe] Working a past paper
> 1. Timer on, everything closed except your card. Write units and a direction on every answer.
> 2. Grade against the official solution. The guide pages here do not repeat the keys.
> 3. Sort each lost point: *did not know the equation* (→ the [[0-toolkit/09-midterm-3-summary|summary]], then your card), *set it up wrong* (→ the type's method below and its twin), *sign or arithmetic* (→ [[0-toolkit/09-midterm-3-summary#10-the-traps-that-cost-the-most-points|the traps]]).
> 4. Redo the missed parts the next day without looking.

## How these pages were checked

- **The papers were re-solved by script** before their guides were written (`tools/build14/checks/exam3_keys.py`: 83 checks covering the computed parts of the four Spring and Fall papers and the four Summer papers; bounce-diagram parts by a time-domain simulation of the line, `tlsim.py`). Concept items with no number to compute were checked by reading, and the 2019 conflict paper was read but not re-solved. 81 of the 83 agree with the official keys. Two are slips in the keys, shown on the guide pages and listed in the [[0-toolkit/04-errata-in-the-course-materials#past-exam-3-solution-keys|errata]]: the load-end level in SP18 #3(d), and an intermediate factor in SP19 #1(d) that does not change its boxed answer.
- **The twins** on the steady-state and Summer pages are computed in `twins.py` and re-derived by a second route in `review_twins.py` (line simulation, the spectrum of a ringing line, a lumped-ladder model): 52 checks, all passing.
- **The point counts** in the papers table: `hub_counts.py`.
- Twins that link into the [[practice/index|practice bank]] were checked when the bank was built, each by its author's script and by an independent re-solution.
- The guides describe what each problem asks and how to do it. They do not reproduce the papers or their keys: work from the PDFs in `Exams/`.
