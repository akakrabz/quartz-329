> [!key] The whole thing in four lines
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> \text{given } \mu,\ \epsilon,\ \sigma,\ \omega:\qquad \gamma = \sqrt{j\omega\mu(\sigma + j\omega\epsilon)} = \alpha + j\beta,\qquad \eta = \sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma + j\omega\epsilon}} = \lvert\eta\rvert e^{j\tau},\\[6pt]
> \gamma\eta = j\omega\mu,\qquad \frac{\gamma}{\eta} = \sigma + j\omega\epsilon,\qquad p\equiv\frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon} = \tan\theta,\qquad \tau = \frac{\theta}{2},\\[6pt]
> \delta = \frac1\alpha,\qquad \lambda = \frac{2\pi}{\beta},\qquad v_p = \frac\omega\beta,\qquad v_g = \frac{d\omega}{d\beta},\qquad \tilde{\mathbf{H}} = \frac{\hat k\times\tilde{\mathbf{E}}}{\eta}.
> \end{gathered}
> $$
> Two complex numbers ($\gamma$, $\eta$) carry everything; every other symbol is a part of one of them or a rewrite of it.

## 1. One row per symbol

| symbol | name | unit | what it measures | lossless ($\sigma = 0$) | good conductor ($p\gg1$) | page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\mu$ | permeability | H/m | how much $\mathbf{B}$ a given $\mathbf{H}$ makes; $\mu_0$ in every wave problem of this course | $\mu_0\mu_r$ | same | [[concepts/permeability\|Permeability]] |
| $\epsilon$ | permittivity | F/m | how much $\mathbf{D}$ a given $\mathbf{E}$ makes; sets the displacement current $j\omega\epsilon\tilde{\mathbf{E}}$ | $\epsilon_0\epsilon_r$ | drops out of $\gamma$, $\eta$ | [[concepts/permittivity\|Permittivity]] |
| $\sigma$ | conductivity | S/m | the conduction current $\sigma\tilde{\mathbf{E}}$ — the only source of loss here | $0$ | dominates | [[concepts/conductors\|Conductors]] |
| $\omega$ | angular frequency | rad/s | $2\pi f$; the knob that moves a medium between regimes | — | — | [[concepts/monochromatic-waves\|Monochromatic waves]] |
| $p = \sigma/\omega\epsilon$ | loss tangent, $\tan\theta$ | — | conduction current ÷ displacement current; $\theta$ = loss angle | $0$ | $\gg1$ | [[concepts/loss-tangent\|Loss tangent]] |
| $\gamma$ | propagation constant | 1/m | the exponent of $e^{-\gamma z}$: decay and phase together | $j\omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ | $(1+j)/\delta$ | [[concepts/propagation-constant\|Propagation constant]] |
| $\alpha$ | attenuation constant | Np/m | $\text{Re}\,\gamma$; amplitude falls as $e^{-\alpha z}$, power as $e^{-2\alpha z}$ | $0$ | $1/\delta$ | [[concepts/propagation-constant\|Propagation constant]] |
| $\beta$ | phase constant | rad/m | $\text{Im}\,\gamma$; phase turns by $\beta$ per metre | $\omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ | $1/\delta$ | [[concepts/monochromatic-waves\|Monochromatic waves]] |
| $\eta$ | intrinsic impedance | Ω | $\tilde E/\tilde H$ of one travelling wave; complex when lossy | $\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$ (real) | $(1+j)/\sigma\delta$ | [[concepts/intrinsic-impedance\|Intrinsic impedance]] |
| $\tau$ | angle of $\eta$ | rad | $\mathbf{H}$ lags $\mathbf{E}$ by $\tau$; $\tau = \theta/2$, $\tan\tau = \alpha/\beta$ | $0$ | $45^\circ$ | [[concepts/intrinsic-impedance\|Intrinsic impedance]] |
| $\delta$ | penetration (skin) depth | m | $1/\alpha$: distance for the amplitude to fall to $e^{-1}$ | $\infty$ | $1/\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}$ | [[concepts/skin-depth\|Skin depth]] |
| $\lambda$ | wavelength | m | $2\pi/\beta$: one full turn of phase | $v_p/f$ | $2\pi\delta$ | [[concepts/monochromatic-waves\|Monochromatic waves]] |
| $v_p$ | phase velocity | m/s | $\omega/\beta$: speed of a crest | $1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon} = c/n$ | $\omega\delta$ | [[concepts/dispersion-relation\|Dispersion relation]] |
| $v_g$ | group velocity | m/s | $d\omega/d\beta$: speed of the envelope (the message) | $= v_p$ if $\beta\propto\omega$ | — | [[concepts/group-velocity\|Group velocity]] |
| $n$ | refractive index | — | $\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r}$; $v_p = c/n$, $\eta = \eta_0/n$, $\beta = n\omega/c$ | $\sqrt{\epsilon_r}$ | — | [[concepts/intrinsic-impedance\|Intrinsic impedance]] |

## 2. Same quantity, five spellings

The identities that make exam questions look different from each other:

$$
\begin{gathered}
\beta = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon} = \frac{\omega}{v_p} = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} = \frac{n\omega}{c} = \frac{2\pi n}{\lambda_0}\qquad(\text{lossless}),\\[6pt]
\eta = \sqrt{\frac\mu\epsilon} = \frac{\eta_0}{n} = \frac{\eta_0}{\sqrt{\epsilon_r}}\ (\mu = \mu_0) = \frac{\omega\mu}{\beta} = \frac{\beta}{\omega\epsilon}\qquad(\text{lossless}),\\[6pt]
\alpha = \frac1\delta,\qquad \tan\tau = \frac\alpha\beta,\qquad \lvert\gamma\rvert = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}\,(1+p^2)^{1/4},\qquad \lvert\eta\rvert = \sqrt{\frac\mu\epsilon}\,(1+p^2)^{-1/4},\\[6pt]
p = \frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon} = \tan\theta = \frac{1}{\omega\tau_r}\ \Big(\tau_r = \frac\epsilon\sigma\Big) = \frac{G}{\omega C}\ (\text{leaky capacitor}) = \frac{f_c}{f}\ \Big(f_c = \frac{\sigma}{2\pi\epsilon}\Big).
\end{gathered}
$$

The lossless middle lines are where most slips happen: $\beta$ carries $\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ (a *product*), $\eta$ carries $\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$ (a *ratio*). Doubling $\epsilon_r$ to 4 halves $\eta$ and halves $v_p$ but doubles $\beta$.

## 3. Reading a wave versus building one

Two directions through the map, and exams ask both.

> [!recipe] Given the wave, find the medium (Lecture 22 §5 — the decoding problem)
> 1. From $e^{-\alpha z}\cos(\omega t - \beta z + \phi)$ read $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\omega$ (a $+\beta z$ means travel toward $-z$).
> 2. $\gamma = \alpha + j\beta$ — write it in polar form at once, $\lvert\gamma\rvert\angle\psi$.
> 3. $\eta = j\omega\mu/\gamma = (\omega\mu/\lvert\gamma\rvert)\angle(90^\circ - \psi)$ — needs $\mu$ (given, almost always $\mu_0$).
> 4. $\sigma + j\omega\epsilon = \gamma/\eta = \gamma^2/j\omega\mu$: real part $\sigma$, imaginary part $\div\omega$ gives $\epsilon$.
> 5. Partner field: $\tilde{\mathbf{H}} = \hat k\times\tilde{\mathbf{E}}/\eta$ (magnitude $\div\lvert\eta\rvert$, phase $-\tau$); $\langle\mathbf{S}\rangle = \hat k\,\tfrac12\lvert E_0\rvert^2 e^{-2\alpha z}\cos\tau/\lvert\eta\rvert$.
>
> Checks: $\psi = 90^\circ - \tau$, so $\psi$ lies between $45^\circ$ (good conductor) and $90^\circ$ (lossless), and $\angle\eta = \tau\le45^\circ$; $\beta\ge\alpha$ in every passive medium, so $\psi<45^\circ$ means $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are swapped.

> [!recipe] Given the medium and $f$, find the wave (Lecture 23 §7)
> 1. $p = \sigma/\omega\epsilon$ with the medium's own $\epsilon = \epsilon_r\epsilon_0$ — not $\epsilon_0$.
> 2. $p < 0.1$: imperfect dielectric formulas. $p > 10$: good-conductor formulas. Between: exact $\gamma$, $\eta$ (polar form, [[concepts/loss-tangent|Loss tangent]]).
> 3. Write $\gamma$, then $\delta = 1/\alpha$, $\lambda = 2\pi/\beta$, $v_p = \omega/\beta$, then $\eta$ for $\mathbf{H}$ and $\langle\mathbf{S}\rangle$.
> 4. Power questions: $e^{-2\alpha z}$, never $e^{-\alpha z}$; in dB, $20\log_{10}e^{\alpha z} = 8.69\,\alpha z$.

## 4. Symbols that collide

| symbol | here it means | elsewhere in the course it means | tell them apart |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\tau$ | $\angle\eta$, half the loss angle, 0–45° | relaxation time $\tau_r = \epsilon/\sigma$ (Lecture 8); collision time in the Drude model (Lecture 11); $\tau = 1+\Gamma$ transmission coefficient (Lecture 25) | an angle in a wave problem; a time in a charge-decay or conductivity problem; a dimensionless ratio next to $\Gamma$ |
| $\theta$ | loss angle, $\tan\theta = \sigma/\omega\epsilon$ (Rao's and the exams' name) | polar angle in spherical coordinates; angle of incidence | — |
| $\delta$ | penetration/skin depth $1/\alpha$ [m] | $\delta(x)$ the delta function (Lecture 3); Rao writes the loss tangent as $\tan\delta$ | the notes never write $\tan\delta$; on this site the loss angle is $\theta$ |
| $\gamma$ | $\alpha + j\beta$ [1/m] | — (the notes keep $\gamma$ for this alone) | — |
| $\beta$ | phase constant [rad/m] | some texts write $k$; $k = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ is the *lossless* $\beta$ | $\hat k$ with a hat is the travel direction, $k$ without one is $\beta$ |
| $\alpha$ | attenuation [Np/m] | — | a Neper is dimensionless: $\alpha z$ is a pure number; $1\ \text{Np} = 8.69\ \text{dB}$ |
| $\eta$ | intrinsic impedance of a *medium* [Ω] | $Z_0$ is the characteristic impedance of a *line* (Lecture 27) — same role, different object | $\eta$ from $\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$; $Z_0$ from $\sqrt{\mathcal{L}/\mathcal{C}}$ |
| $v_p$ vs $v_g$ | crest speed $\omega/\beta$ vs envelope speed $d\omega/d\beta$ | — | equal only when $\beta\propto\omega$ (no dispersion); plasma: $v_pv_g = c^2$ |
| $\epsilon$ in $p$ | the medium's $\epsilon_r\epsilon_0$ | — | seawater: $\epsilon_r = 81$, so $p = \sigma/(81\omega\epsilon_0)$ |

## 5. The regimes side by side

| | perfect dielectric | imperfect dielectric $p\ll1$ | good conductor $p\gg1$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\alpha$ | $0$ | $\dfrac\sigma2\sqrt{\dfrac\mu\epsilon}$ (frequency independent) | $\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma} = 1/\delta$ |
| $\beta$ | $\omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ | $\omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ | $\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma} = 1/\delta$ |
| $\lvert\eta\rvert$, $\tau$ | $\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$, $0$ | $\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$, $p/2$ | $\sqrt{\omega\mu/\sigma}$, $45^\circ$ |
| $\delta$ | $\infty$ | $\dfrac2\sigma\sqrt{\dfrac\epsilon\mu}$ | $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}}$ — grows as $f$ falls |
| $\lambda$ | $v_p/f$ | $v_p/f$ | $2\pi\delta$ — tiny |
| $v_p$ | $1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ | $1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$ | $\omega\delta$ — slow, dispersive |
| wavelengths per $\delta$ | — | many ($\approx 1/\pi p$) | $1/2\pi$: dead within a wavelength |
| what controls loss | — | $\sigma$ alone | $f$ and $\sigma$ together |

What each limit *drops*: the imperfect dielectric keeps $j\omega\epsilon$ and treats $\sigma$ as a small correction (binomial expansion); the good conductor drops $j\omega\epsilon$ entirely, which is why $\epsilon$ never appears in its formulas. Derivations: [[concepts/loss-tangent|Loss tangent]]; the conductor side: [[concepts/skin-depth|Skin depth]].
