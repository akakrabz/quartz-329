> [!key] Fields radiated by a current sheet
> A uniform $\mathbf{J}_s(t)$ [A/m] on an infinite plane, in a lossless medium ($\eta = \sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$, $v = 1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$), radiates
> $$
> \mathbf{E} = -\frac\eta2\,\mathbf{J}_s\Big(t - \frac dv\Big),\qquad \mathbf{H} = \frac12\,\mathbf{J}_s\Big(t - \frac dv\Big)\times\hat n,\qquad \mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H}\parallel+\hat n .
> $$
> $d$: distance from the sheet; $\hat n$: unit normal from the sheet toward the field point (flips side to side). Notes' form, $\mathbf{J}_s = \hat x\,f(t)$ on $z = 0$:
> $$
> \mathbf{E}^\pm = -\hat x\,\frac{\eta}{2}\,f\Big(t\mp\frac zv\Big),\qquad \mathbf{H}^\pm = \mp\hat y\,\frac12\,f\Big(t\mp\frac zv\Big)\qquad\text{in } z\gtrless0 .
> $$
> Upper signs together ($z>0$), lower together. **No** $\mp$ before $\mathbf{E}$. Amplitudes $\tfrac\eta2\lvert J_s\rvert$ and $\tfrac12\lvert J_s\rvert$ (ratio $\eta$); in vacuum 1 A/m gives $188$ V/m ($60\pi$) and 0.5 A/m per side.

**Physics (notes' route).** A *plane* wave needs a source uniform over a plane: a sheet $\mathbf{J}_s = \hat x\,J_x$ [A/m] on $z = 0$, homogeneous lossless medium on both sides.

*Step 1: static current* ([[concepts/current-sheets-and-slabs|Current sheets and slabs]]):

$$
\mathbf{H} = \tfrac12\mathbf{J}_s\times\hat n = \mp\hat y\,\frac{J_x}{2}\quad(z\gtrless0),
$$

$\hat n$ toward the field point ($\tfrac12J_x\,\hat x\times\hat z = -\tfrac12J_x\hat y$ above); $\mathbf{H}$ jumps by $J_x$; no $\mathbf{E}$.

*Step 2: quasi-static.* For slowly varying $J_x(t)$, $\mathbf{H}\approx\mp\hat y\,J_x(t)/2$ near the sheet — everywhere would mean infinite speed (the notes' footnote 1); valid only for negligible $\lvert z\rvert/v$.

*Step 3: delay it (exact).* Off the sheet the fields are source-free d'Alembert waves: $J_x(t)\to J_x(t\mp z/v)$, "≈" → "=":

$$
\mathbf{H}(z,t) = \mp\hat y\,\frac12J_x\Big(t\mp\frac zv\Big),\qquad \mathbf{E}(z,t) = -\hat x\,\frac\eta2\,J_x\Big(t\mp\frac zv\Big)\qquad(z\gtrless0).
$$

$\mathbf{E}$ follows from the [[concepts/plane-waves|plane-wave]] rule with $\mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H}$ away from the sheet: $(-\hat x)\times(\mp\hat y) = \pm\hat z$ ✓. **Causality picks the signs** (the notes' footnote 2): both $t\mp z/v$ solve the wave equation, but the sheet is the only source, so waves travel only away from it: $t - \lvert z\rvert/v$. **Uniqueness**: d'Alembert waves meeting the jump conditions are the one solution. **The displacement current** makes Step 2 inexact: without $\partial\mathbf{D}/\partial t$, no $\mathbf{E}$ and infinite speed; it couples $\mathbf{H}$ to $\mathbf{E}$ and delays the response by $\lvert z\rvert/v$.

> [!derivation]- The same result from boundary conditions (slides) — reproduce this when asked to "derive"
> Slides: $\mathbf{J}_s = -J_s(t)\,\hat x$, free space ($\eta_0$, $v_p = c$); site notation:
> 1. **General solution:** $E_x = A f(t - z/v) + B g(t + z/v)$, $H_y = \frac{1}{\eta}\big[Af(t - z/v) - Bg(t + z/v)\big]$.
> 2. **Radiation condition:** $B = 0$ for $z>0$, $A = 0$ for $z<0$: $E_x = Af$, $H_y = Af/\eta$ above; $E_x = Bg$, $H_y = -Bg/\eta$ below.
> 3. **Tangential $\mathbf{E}$ continuous** at $z = 0$: $Af(t) = Bg(t)$ for all $t$; same waveform, same sign.
> 4. **Tangential $\mathbf{H}$ jumps by $\mathbf{J}_s$:** $\hat n\times(\mathbf{H}_1 - \mathbf{H}_2) = \mathbf{J}_s$, region 1 = $z>0$, $\hat n = \hat z$. With $\hat z\times\hat y = -\hat x$, $-\hat x\,\big[H_y(0^+) - H_y(0^-)\big] = -J_s\hat x$:
> $$
> H_y(0^+) - H_y(0^-) = \frac{Af(t)}{\eta} - \Big(-\frac{Af(t)}{\eta}\Big) = \frac{2Af(t)}{\eta} = J_s(t)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Af(t) = \frac\eta2\,J_s(t).
> $$
> 5. **Delay it:** $Af(t - z/v) = \tfrac\eta2J_s(t - z/v)$; likewise below.
>
> Blue ink (slides 5, 7): the sign-slip spots, steps 3–4.

**The slides' form (free space).** For $\mathbf{J}_s = -J_s(t)\,\hat x$ on $z = 0$:

$$
\mathbf{E}(z,t) = \hat x\,\frac{\eta_0}{2}\,J_s\Big(t\mp\frac zc\Big),\qquad \mathbf{H}(z,t) = \pm\hat y\,\frac12\,J_s\Big(t\mp\frac zc\Big),\qquad z\gtrless0 .
$$
The notes' result with $f\to-J_s$ (the sheets point opposite ways); in both, $\mathbf{E}$ opposes the current.

> [!recipe] Fields of a current sheet in any orientation
> 1. Write $\mathbf{J}_s(t)$ as a vector, sign included.
> 2. $\mathbf{H} = \tfrac12\mathbf{J}_s\times\hat n$ each side, $\hat n$ toward the field point.
> 3. $\mathbf{E} = -\tfrac\eta2\mathbf{J}_s$, the same on both sides.
> 4. $t\to t - d/v$ (sheet on $y = 0$: $t\mp y/v$, $y\gtrless0$).
> 5. Check $\mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H}$ away from the sheet on both sides.

**Reading the result.** **E is even, H is odd** (circled on the slides: $\eta_0/2$ both sides, $\pm\tfrac12$ flips) — the [[concepts/boundary-conditions|boundary conditions]] $E_x(0^+,t) = E_x(0^-,t)$ and $\hat z\times(\mathbf{H}^+ - \mathbf{H}^-) = \hat z\times(-\hat y\,f) = +\hat x\,f = \mathbf{J}_s$ (notes' orientation); the $\tfrac12$ is the static half-jump per side. **E opposes the current:** $\mathbf{J}_s\cdot\mathbf{E} = -\tfrac\eta2\lvert\mathbf{J}_s\rvert^2<0$ — the charges work on the field, so the sheet *sources* $\tfrac\eta2J_s^2$ per unit area ([[concepts/poynting-vector|Poynting vector]]), seeing two half-spaces of $\eta$ in parallel: $\eta/2$ ([[concepts/intrinsic-impedance|Intrinsic impedance]]).

**A sinusoidal sheet (slides).** $\mathbf{J}_s = -J_{s0}\cos(\omega t)\,\hat x$ on $z = 0$, free space; $J_s(t\mp z/c) = J_{s0}\cos(\omega t\mp\beta z)$:

$$
\mathbf{E}(z,t) = \hat x\,\frac{\eta_0J_{s0}}{2}\cos(\omega t\mp\beta z),\qquad \mathbf{H}(z,t) = \pm\hat y\,\frac{J_{s0}}{2}\cos(\omega t\mp\beta z),\qquad z\gtrless0,\qquad \beta = \frac{\omega}{v_p}.
$$
Real cosines; $\omega$, $f$, $\beta$, $\lambda$, $v_p$ from the phase: [[concepts/plane-waves|Plane waves]]. Power density, outward both sides, never negative ($\cos^2\ge0$):
$$
\mathbf{S} = \mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H} = \pm\hat z\,\frac{\eta_0J_{s0}^2}{4}\cos^2(\omega t\mp\beta z)\ \ \text{W/m}^2,\qquad z\gtrless0 .
$$
Peak $\eta_0/4 = 94.2$ W/m² per side for $J_{s0} = 1$ A/m; both sides together $\eta_0J_s^2/2 = -\mathbf{J}_s\cdot\mathbf{E}$ ([[concepts/poynting-vector|Poynting vector]], [[concepts/poynting-theorem|Poynting's theorem]]).

**Examples.**

> [!example] Notes, Example 4 (Lecture 19) — a ramp of sheet current at $t = 2$ µs
> Free space, $\mathbf{J}_s = \hat x\,f(t)$ on $z = 0$, $f(t) = At\,\mathrm{rect}(t/\tau)$, $A = 2$ A/m per µs, $\tau = 1$ µs (ramp from $-1$ to $+1$ A/m); $c = 300$ m/µs, $\eta_0\approx120\pi\ \Omega$. The waves have gone 600 m: fields in $450<\lvert z\rvert<750$ m,
> $$
> H_y(z, 2\ \mu\text{s}) = \mp\frac12\,f\Big(2\mp\frac{z}{300}\Big),\qquad E_x(z, 2\ \mu\text{s}) = -60\pi\,f\Big(2\mp\frac z{300}\Big)\quad(z\gtrless0;\ z\ \text{in m},\ f\ \text{in A/m}).
> $$
> - $z>0$ (450 → 750 m): $H_y$ rises $-0.5\to+0.5$ A/m; $E_x$ rises $-60\pi\to+60\pi\approx188$ V/m.
> - $z<0$ ($-750\to-450$ m): $H_y$ rises $-0.5\to+0.5$; $E_x$ falls $+60\pi\to-60\pi$.
>
> Delayed copies of the source, mirrored on the $+z$ side. Check, 700 m: $f(2 - 7/3) = f(-1/3) = -2/3$ A/m, $H_y = +1/3$ A/m, $E_x = +40\pi$ V/m, $\hat x\times\hat y = +\hat z$ ✓.

> [!example] Slide 17 — given $\mathbf{J}_s$ and $\mathbf{H}$ of a sheet on $y = 0$, find $\mathbf{E}$
> Sheet in the $xz$-plane, $\mathbf{J}_s = -J_s(t)\,\hat z$, sinusoidal $\mathbf{H}$ of amplitude $H_0$ (unsolved in the deck).
> - $\hat n = \pm\hat y$ for $y\gtrless0$: $\mathbf{H} = \tfrac12(-J_s\hat z)\times(\pm\hat y) = \pm\tfrac12J_s\,\hat x$, so $H_0 = J_{s0}/2$.
> - $\mathbf{E} = -\tfrac{\eta_0}{2}\mathbf{J}_s = +\hat z\,\tfrac{\eta_0}{2}J_s$ both sides, against the current, amplitude $\eta_0H_0\approx377H_0$ ($120\pi H_0$); $\hat z\times(\pm\hat x) = \pm\hat y$ ✓.
> - Cosine current: $\mathbf{E} = \hat z\,\eta_0H_0\cos(\omega t\mp\beta y)$, $\mathbf{H} = \pm\hat x\,H_0\cos(\omega t\mp\beta y)$, $y\gtrless0$.

> [!example] Notes, Example 1 (Lecture 20) — a cosine current sheet in vacuum
> $\mathbf{J}_s = \hat x\,f(t) = \hat x\,2\cos(\omega t)$ A/m on $z = 0$: $\mathbf{E} = -\hat x\,\tfrac\eta2f(t\mp z/v)$, $\mathbf{H} = \mp\hat y\,\tfrac12f(t\mp z/v)$ with $v = c$, $\eta = \eta_0$; delay shifts phase:
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> f\Big(t\mp\frac zc\Big) = 2\cos\Big(\omega t\mp\frac{\omega}{c}z\Big) = 2\cos(\omega t\mp\beta z),\\[4pt]
> \beta\equiv\frac\omega c .
> \end{gathered}
> $$
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> \mathbf{E}(z,t) = -\hat x\,\eta_0\cos(\omega t\mp\beta z)\ \ \text{V/m},\\[4pt]
> \mathbf{H}(z,t) = \mp\hat y\,\cos(\omega t\mp\beta z)\ \ \text{A/m},\quad z\gtrless0 .
> \end{gathered}
> $$
> Amplitudes $\eta_0\cdot2/2 = \eta_0\approx377$ V/m, $2/2 = 1$ A/m ([[concepts/monochromatic-waves|monochromatic waves]]). Poynting: $z>0$, $(-\eta_0\cos)(-\cos)\,\hat x\times\hat y = +\hat z\,\eta_0\cos^2(\omega t - \beta z)$; $z<0$, $(-\eta_0\cos)(+\cos)\,\hat x\times\hat y = -\hat z\,\eta_0\cos^2(\omega t + \beta z)$:
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> \mathbf{S} = \mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H} = \pm\hat z\,\eta_0\cos^2(\omega t\mp\beta z)\ \ \text{W/m}^2,\\[4pt]
> z\gtrless0 .
> \end{gathered}
> $$
> Pulses at $2\omega$, humps $\lambda/2$ apart. On the sheet $\mathbf{J}_s\cdot\mathbf{E} = -2\eta_0\cos^2(\omega t)$ W/m² ([[concepts/poynting-theorem|Poynting's theorem]]); averages $\eta_0/2\approx188$ W/m² per side, $\eta_0\approx377$ W/m² injected ([[concepts/time-average-power|Time averages and average power]]).

Below, the **plot's scale** ($H_y = 0.02x$, peak 2 A/m); for the formula, multiply fields and current by 100.

The vertical edge at 100 m is the front, reaching 400 m $300/300 = 1$ µs later: the record jumps to 2 A/m at $t = 2$ µs, falls linearly to 0 at $t = 7/3\approx2.33$ µs, then stays zero (sharp edge first in time).
> - **(b) $H_y(x, 4\ \mu\text{s})$, $\lvert x\rvert\le1500$ m.** 900 m further on: $x>0$, ramp from 0 at 900 m to 2 A/m at 1000 m, then zero; $x<0$, reversed (odd): step to $-2$ A/m at $-1000$ m, ramping to 0 at $-900$ m.
> - **(c) $\mathbf{E}(x, 2\ \mu\text{s})$, $\lvert x\rvert\le500$ m.** $\mathbf{E} = \eta_0\mathbf{H}\times\hat x$ along $\hat y\times\hat x = -\hat z$ for $x>0$; even. Front at $\lvert x\rvert = 400$ m: $E_z = -2\eta_0(\lvert x\rvert - 300)/100$ for $300<\lvert x\rvert<400$ m, 0 to $-2\eta_0\approx-754$ V/m at $\lvert x\rvert\to400$ m, zero elsewhere.
> - **(d) The current.** $\hat n = \hat x$: $\mathbf{J}_s = \hat x\times\big(\mathbf{H}(0^+) - \mathbf{H}(0^-)\big) = \hat z\,2H_y(0^+, t)$, $H_y(0^+, t) = F\big(c(1 - t)\big)$, nonzero for $2/3<t<1$ µs:
> $$
> \mathbf{J}_s(t) = \hat z\,12\Big(1 - \frac{t}{1\ \mu\text{s}}\Big)\ \text{A/m}\quad\text{for } \tfrac23\ \mu\text{s}<t<1\ \mu\text{s},\ \ \text{zero otherwise}.
> $$

**In a conducting medium** (Lecture 22 §7): $j\beta\to\gamma$, complex $\eta$: $\tilde{\mathbf{E}} = -\tfrac\eta2\tilde{\mathbf{J}}_s\,e^{-\gamma d}$, $\tilde{\mathbf{H}} = \tfrac12\tilde{\mathbf{J}}_s\times\hat n\,e^{-\gamma d}$ — decaying waves, $\mathbf{E}$ leading $\mathbf{H}$ by the phase of $\eta$; worked on [[concepts/intrinsic-impedance|Intrinsic impedance]].

> [!trap] Sign traps at a sheet
> - A $\mp$ before $\mathbf{E}$ by analogy with $\mathbf{H}$: tangential $\mathbf{E}$ would jump. Only $\mathbf{H}$ flips.
> - $t + z/v$ above the sheet: the argument is always $t - (\text{distance})/v$, outgoing.
> - One $\hat n$ for both sides: it points toward the field point, $+\hat z$ above, $-\hat z$ below.
> - Dropping the $\tfrac12$: each side gets $\eta J_s/2$, $J_s/2$, not $\eta J_s$, $J_s$.
> - Slides' and notes' sheets point opposite ways; compare $\mathbf{E}$ with $\mathbf{J}_s$, not formula with formula.
> - A probe records the source waveform delayed; a snapshot against distance shows it mirrored (front farthest out), against the coordinate mirrored on the $+$ side only.
