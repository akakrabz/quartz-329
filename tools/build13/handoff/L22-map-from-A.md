## 3. Conducting media: one replacement
Slides 21–22a, slides 2–5, 9–12: $\sigma$, $\epsilon$, $\mu$ in one material, $\nabla\times\mathbf{H} = \sigma\mathbf{E} + \epsilon\,\partial\mathbf{E}/\partial t$; the planar phasor pair gives $\partial^2\tilde E_x/\partial z^2 = \bar\gamma^2\tilde E_x$, $\bar\gamma = \sqrt{j\omega\mu(\sigma + j\omega\epsilon)}$ (overbar = complex) → [[concepts/phasor-maxwell-equations|Maxwell's equations in phasor form]]

## 4. The damped plane wave
Slides 21–22a, slides 13–14, 21–23: $Ae^{-\alpha z}\cos(\omega t - \beta z + \theta)$ with $\mathbf{H}$ divided by $\lvert\bar\eta\rvert$ and lagging by $\tau$; slide 21's ink strikes "Np/m or dB/m" as units of $\alpha$ (1/m = Np/m; dB/m is $8.686\alpha$, see errata) → [[concepts/current-sheet-radiation|Radiation from a current sheet]]

## 7. Lecture 19's current sheet in a conducting medium
Slides 21–22a, slides 8–25: the cosine sheet in $(\sigma, \mu, \epsilon)$ by boundary conditions, $A = \lvert\eta\rvert J_{s0}/2$, $\theta = \tau$; $\mathbf{E}$ leads $\mathbf{H}$ by $\tau$ (crests $\tau/\beta\le\lambda/8$ apart); challenge (slide 25): $\sigma = 0$, $\epsilon = 2\epsilon_0$ — none of attenuation, phase offset, speed $c$ → [[concepts/current-sheet-radiation|Radiation from a current sheet]]
