**Physics (Lecture 3 §6).** Magnetic field lines have no ends: they close or run on forever. The slides' "Yadaraf bugs" (Faraday backwards) always return to their hole, so any *closed* net catches as many leaving as returning. The second Maxwell equation:

> [!key] Gauss's law for magnetism
> $$
> \psi_B = \oint_S \mathbf{B}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = 0 \quad\text{for every closed surface } S.
> $$
> No magnetic charge. An *open* surface can have nonzero magnetic flux (Faraday's law, [[2-magnetostatics/14-faradays-law-and-induced-emf|Lecture 14]]); a *closed* one never does.

Shrinking the surface to a point gives $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B} = 0$ ([[concepts/divergence]]): no magnetic sources or sinks anywhere.
