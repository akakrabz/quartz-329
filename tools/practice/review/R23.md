## Findings and changes (0 blockers, 1 major, 4 minors)
1. **major**: 23.9 Check said the phasor fields "satisfy Faraday's law and the Ampère–Maxwell law (checked numerically)". The
   good-conductor phasors satisfy Faraday exactly (γη = jωμ) but Ampère–Maxwell only to 1.1 % (ωε/|σ+jωε| ≈ 1/90, the dropped
   displacement current). Reworded to say so. No answer was affected.
2. **minor**: 23.9 Check put "τ = 44.7°" inside "within 0.6 % of the shortcuts". τ is 0.71 % (0.3°) below 45°; only δ and λ are
   within 0.56 %. Reworded to "τ = 44.7° instead of 45°".
3. **minor**: 23.10 Check said "to within 0.001 %". At 25 kHz the exact α and β differ from the GC values by 0.0014 % (≈ 1/2p).
   Changed to 0.002 %.
4. **minor**: 23.5(e) did not give ε_r, although the solution confirms the regime with ε = 81ε₀. Added "ε_r = 81" to the statement.
5. **minor**: 23.12 Check said "the magnetic field no longer penetrates the copper". Changed to "now reaches only about a skin depth into the copper".
