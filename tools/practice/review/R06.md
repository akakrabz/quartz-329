## Findings (0 blocker, 0 major, 4 minor; all fixed)

1. **minor, 6.1 distractor (a).** "Normal **E** jumps at any charged surface" overstates the case. Here ε₁ ≠ ε₂, so for
   ρ_s = 4ε₀ the special field E₂ₙ = ρ_s/(ε₁−ε₂) = −4/3 V/m has a continuous normal E (see the note line in R06.out). Changed to
   "In general normal **E** jumps at a charged surface". The key is unaffected.
2. **minor, 6.1 distractor (e).** "One component jumps, so the magnitudes differ" does not hold in general:
   E₁ₙ = −E₂ₙ = 4/7 V/m gives |E₁| = |E₂| (note line in R06.out). Reworded to "the normal component changes while the
   tangential part does not, so in general the magnitudes differ; here |E₁| = 7.40 V/m against |E₂| = 2.57 V/m".
3. **minor, 6.8(e) statement.** The water surface's free charge was not stated, and the tan-ratio law needs ρ_s = 0. Added
   "which carries no free charge". The answer 0.71° is unchanged.
4. **minor, 6.8 hint.** "With no free charge, E_t is the same on both sides and so is εE_n" can be read as making E_t
   continuity depend on ρ_s = 0, which contradicts the lesson of 6.1. Reworded to "E_t is the same on both sides of any interface,
   and with no free charge so is εE_n".
