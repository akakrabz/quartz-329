## Findings (no blockers, no majors; 3 minors, all fixed)
1. **Minor, 21.11(d).** The solution put the swing of ⟨w_e⟩ at "0.319 and 1.28 nJ/m³". 16ε = 144ε₀ is 1.2732 nJ/m³ with the problem's own data (c = 3×10⁸ and η₀ = 120π, so ε₀ = 1/(η₀c)), and 1.27498 with the ε₀ = 8.854×10⁻¹² used elsewhere on the page. Only full-precision SI ε₀ gives 1.27500, which rounds to 1.28. Likewise 4ε is 0.318 or 0.319 depending on ε₀. **Changed** to "about 0.32 and 1.27 nJ/m³", which holds under both conventions (printed by R21.py). The Answer line, 20ε ≈ 1.59 nJ/m³, was already correct.
2. **Minor, 21.10(f).** The statement asked "Why do the two sheets' powers no longer simply add?". But the total, 75π, still equals the stand-alone 60π + 15π; only the split between the two sides interferes, which is what the solution explains. **Changed** to "…no longer simply add on each side?".
3. **Minor, 21.12(a).** The second way of reading the direction (the phase rises by 45° toward +z) silently relies on the spacing being under λ/2; otherwise the reading could be a 315° lag toward +z with β ≥ 7π. **Added** "the probes are closer than λ/2, so this +45° cannot be a 315° lag". R21.py enumerates all allowed (direction, β): only (−z, π).
