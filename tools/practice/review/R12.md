  `R12.py` confirms B_old(Rp) = R·B_exam(p) to 9e-15. So the exam's null line x = −2y (in z = 0) carried over
  as the page's (c) answer z = −x/2, and R maps the exam's null points (2,−1,0) and (−2,1,0) onto the page's
  examples (−2,0,1) and (2,0,−1).
  **Fixed:** the currents are now **3 A along +ẑ** (z wire) and **1 A along −x̂** (x wire); P and v are unchanged.
  I re-derived and re-verified (a)–(d), the Check and the Answer:
