## Findings (all fixed in the page)

- **M1 (major, misleading hint) 5.1.** The hint said "Only motion *along* E costs work against the field". For a
  positive test charge, motion along E is downhill: the field does the work. On a sign question this
  points toward distractor (a), +150 V. Now: "Only the part of the displacement *along* E matters; a step
  perpendicular to E changes nothing."
- **M2 (major, misleading claim) 5.2(b).** The solution said "the electron is attracted toward [P]: the agent has to hold it back the
  whole way". No path is specified, and only the total work is path-independent. R05.py finds
  V(0.7, 0.05, 0) = −270 V, so a route that passes close to Q2 raises U to +270 eV and the agent must *push* there.
  The radial route is monotonic, so the statement holds only for that route. Reworded: qV ends 18 eV lower,
  so "on balance" the agent holds the electron back.
- **m1 (minor) 5.1(d).** "all give the same 150 V" was next to the keyed −150 V. Now "the same ∫E·dl = 150 V".
- **m2 (minor) 5.3(b).** E = 2q/(4πε₀d²) used q and d without defining them. Added "with q = 1 nC and d = 10 cm"
  (value printed by R05.py).
